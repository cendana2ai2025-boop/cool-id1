Privacy Policy

A transparent overview of how your data is handled within the Luminous environment. We prioritize security and absolute minimal collection.

policy 1. Data Collection Architecture

In our pursuit of creating a seamless digital experience, we adhere strictly to a minimalist approach regarding data telemetry. The Luminous Architect system collects only essential navigational metrics required to ensure the stability and performance of our infrastructure.

  • Functional Telemetry: Anonymized interaction paths to optimize interface rendering.
  • Performance Metrics: Load times, error rates, and resource utilization to maintain a 4K editorial experience.

2. Third-Party Integrations

We do not employ ubiquitous tracking pixels or pervasive advertising networks. Integrations are strictly limited to core functional requirements.

Analytics

Self-hosted, privacy-first analytics solution ensuring data never leaves our sovereign servers.

Typography

Cached font delivery to prevent cross-site request tracking via external foundries.

cookie 3. The "No-Cookie" Protocol

Unlike traditional platforms, Luminous operates on a strictly "stateless" methodology for anonymous visitors. We do not store persistent tracking cookies on your device.

info

Local storage is utilized exclusively for interface preferences, such as your dark mode setting or volume controls for ambient audio features, ensuring these preferences remain strictly on your client hardware.

4. Contact & Inquiries

Should you require a deeper technical audit of our data handling protocols, direct communication is available.
